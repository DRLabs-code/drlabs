## 研究結論

Bitcoin and Ethereum are not the same book. Bitcoin holds the reserve book: as of 10 Sep 2026 00:03 UTC, price about **$78,291**, circulating cap about **$1.57T**, rank **1**; hard cap **21.00M**, circulating about **20.08M** (about **96%**). Same-day Ethereum cap about **$301.1B**, roughly **19.2%** of Bitcoin; Solana about **$59.5B**, about **3.8%**. It does not hold the on-chain book: Bitcoin DeFi TVL about **$4.24B**, cap/TVL about **371×**. Ethereum lockup in the same band is about **$50B**, stables about **$146.9B**. Buying BTC is buying reserve and a hard cap, not deposits parked on-chain.

Price is about **38%** below the 6 Oct 2025 ATH of **$126,080**. Thirty days **+22.5%**; seven days only **+1.3%** — the easy leg is done. 24h volume about **$34.94B**, turnover about **2.2%**. The tape is thick, but versus Ethereum’s same-day **~4.4%** and Solana’s **~5.3%** it trades like a reserve, not a ticket.

**Buy score 6.6 / 10 (cautious watch).** Hard cap, liquidity and attention set a floor. Chain fees and the 30-day bounce cap the upside. Not a sell order.

## 主なリスク

- **Wrong denominator:** 371× cap/TVL invites an “overvalued” story. Bitcoin is not priced on lockup.
- **Bounce already printed:** +22.5% in 30 days; seven days only +1.3%.
- **Fees are a rounding error:** ~$6.7M of 30-day chain fees, $0 revenue. Not a cash cow.
- **Dollars are not on this chain:** stables and DEX live on Ethereum / Solana / Tron.
- **Cycle drawdown:** still about 38% below ATH. Reserve assets draw down too.
