## Research take

Solana and Ethereum are not the same object. Solana holds the execution book: 30-day DEX volume about **$72.48B** versus Ethereum **$38.78B**, roughly **1.87×**; 30-day chain fees about **$22.70M** versus Ethereum **$11.38M**. It does not hold the settlement book: chain DeFi TVL about **$5.88B**, roughly **6.7%** of all chains, versus Ethereum **$50.08B** (about **56.8%**); stables about **$16.15B** versus Ethereum **$146.8B**. Market cap about **$60.00B** is about **19.5%** of Ethereum’s $307.1B. Buying SOL is buying turnover, not parked dollars.

Price about **$102.3**, roughly **65%** below the 19 Jan 2025 ATH of **$293.31**. Thirty days **+35.7%**; seven days only **+0.6%** — the easy leg is done. No hard cap; circulating about **586.6M** versus total **633.9M**. LunarCrush social dominance **13.80%** against market-cap dominance of only **2.30%** — discussion is much thicker than the cap. AltIndex last published $SOL cashtags on **20 Jul 2026** at about 362/day; that print cannot be sat next to the September Bitcoin sample.

**Buy score 6.4 / 10 (hold / wait).** Execution sets a floor. Settlement and the 30-day bounce cap the upside. Not a sell order.

## Execution: volume and fees already ahead of Ethereum

30-day DEX: Solana **$72.48B**, Ethereum **$38.78B**, BSC **$36.66B**, Base **$27.06B**. 24h follows the same order: Solana about **$2.92B**, Ethereum **$1.42B**. Fees rhyme: Solana about **$22.70M** over 30 days, Ethereum **$11.38M**. Revenue is Ethereum about **$2.63M** versus Solana **$2.35M** — Ethereum’s capture is not worse, but Solana is the busy venue.

Turnover is thick too: SOL 24h volume about **$4.65B** on a $60B cap is about **7.8%**; Bitcoin about **2.3%**, BNB about **1.2%**, Ethereum about **8.1%**. Versus Ethereum the tape is close; versus larger-cap Bitcoin and BNB, Solana is clearly busier.

## Settlement: lockup and dollars are still a fraction of Ethereum

Chain TVL: Ethereum about **$50.08B**, Solana **$5.88B**, about **one-eighth**. Stables: Ethereum about **$146.8B**, Tron about **$93.8B**, Solana **$16.15B**. Dollars park on Ethereum and Tron, not on Solana. Market cap / TVL about **10.2×** versus Ethereum about **6.1×** — against its own lockup, SOL is more expensive.

The 30-day coin move, +36% versus Ethereum +34%, is almost the same tape. Winning execution has not become a price that is independent of Ethereum. SOL is still a high-turnover risk asset, not a second dollar book.

Take: anyone adding “DEX first” straight onto SOL’s multiple needs stables and TVL to leave “a fraction of Ethereum.” They have not.

## X: social share is much thicker than cap share

LunarCrush (12 Sep): $SOL social dominance **13.80%**, above the 11.20% daily average, against market-cap dominance of only **2.30%**. Discussion is about **6×** the cap share. Sentiment 84%. Support themes are ecosystem expansion (about 55%); critique is cap-share squeeze (about 45%). High-engagement handles include @solana, @toly and Raydium — dense in-circle chatter, not a one-off rumor.

AltIndex last published cashtags on **20 Jul** at about 362/day. The Bitcoin/Ethereum prints in that set are old too, and **must not** sit on the same table as 10 Sep $ETH 198 / $BTC 356. Same-day attention is the LunarCrush dominance print. Sharpe (11 Sep) Google Trends: Bitcoin 56/100, Solana and Ethereum both 8/100 — search interest is still a Bitcoin tape.

Take: SOL is louder on X than its cap. Part of the 30-day bounce is attention premium. Search is not an independent trend, so social dominance is not a fundamental step-up.

## Main risks

- **Settlement does not follow volume:** stables are about 11% of Ethereum, TVL about 12%. The execution premium can be taken apart.
- **Same tape as ETH:** +36% versus ETH +34% over 30 days. No independent price.
- **No hard cap:** circulating is already about 92.5% of current supply, but there is still no ceiling.
- **Social heat prepaid:** 13.8% dominance versus 2.3% cap means discussion is thicker than the book; it can fall faster too.
- **Stale AltIndex series:** the 20 Jul cashtag is not today’s heat.

## What to track

1. Whether Solana / Ethereum 30-day DEX volume stays above 1.5×.
2. Whether stables leave the $16B zone and move toward $30B.
3. Whether chain-level 30-day fees hold above $20M.
4. Whether LunarCrush social dominance falls back toward the 2.3% cap share.
5. Whether SOL and ETH 30-day returns stay glued together.
