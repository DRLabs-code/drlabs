## Market structure: still pinned under its 200-day average

BDX trades at $0.0727, about 10% under its 200-day average and 3% under the 50-day. Under the averages, any good news on BDX first has to absorb trapped supply overhead.

BDX did $10.57M in 24h volume, only about 1.8% turnover (peer median about 6.3%). Institutional size would move BDX's price, which is itself a reason for a discount.

From its $0.451 ATH on 2018-12-16, BDX is down 84%. BDX's last-cycle story has been repriced; a new one needs new data.

For positioning: BDX's price structure is weak; bottom-fishing has poor odds.
