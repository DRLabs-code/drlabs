## Conclusión

A meme ticket is three prints: who is still traded, who is still talked about, and whether any on-chain balance is more than a slogan. SHIB loses all three. Circulating cap is about **$3.02B**, rank 33, still above PEPE’s about **$1.38B**. 24h volume is about **$81.9M** for SHIB, **$274M** for PEPE, **$809M** for DOGE. Turnover is roughly **2.7% / 6.2% / 19.9%**. Second-tier cap, third-tier tape.

The chain is thinner. DefiLlama ShibaSwap is about **$5.98M** on Ethereum and **$27.9k** on Shibarium. Cap / protocol TVL is about **500×**. LunarCrush (11 Sep) social dominance is **0.214%**, under its own 0.643% daily average, and under DOGE **1.00%** and PEPE **0.453%**; creators are about **−68%** year-over-year. On X the usable themes are burns and “the army” on one side, “Shibarium TVL crashed” and “PEPE/BONK/FLOKI took the liquidity” on the other. The second sentence matches TVL and volume.

**Buy score 4.4 / 10 (avoid).** 4.4 is where fundamentals and attention sit, not a call on tomorrow’s print. Not a sell order.

## Cinta: PEPE está más ocupado, DOGE es más grande

30-day SHIB is about **+16.9%**, behind Ethereum’s about **+33.8%** and even behind PEPE’s about **+20.0%**. 7-day SHIB **−1.7%**, PEPE **−6.7%**, DOGE **−0.8%**. It is not the worst drawdown. That is low turnover, not a separate bid.

## On-chain: el relato del burn no cuadra con el TVL

ShibaSwap is the sourced protocol balance: about **$5.98M** on Ethereum, **$27.9k** on Shibarium. The latter is an empty book. LunarCrush lists “Shibarium TVL crash ~74%” as a critical theme (~26%). That matches this TVL. Unverified burn-rate headlines are not used. Tokens moved to a burn address that do not cut tradable float or create fees are an attention prop.

There is no protocol-revenue print. About $3.0B of cap on about $6.0M of app TVL is roughly **500×**. Uniswap sits near 1× TVL; Ethereum near 6×. Almost all of SHIB’s price is brand memory.

Take: Shibarium is not a second fundamental pillar. Until TVL is back above ten million and stays there on DefiLlama, treat the chain book as empty.
