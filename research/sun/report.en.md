## On-chain usage: growing its fees

On DefiLlama, SUN took $1.63M in fees over 30 days, +93% vs the prior 30; at the last 7 days' pace the monthly run-rate is -28%, about $19.77M annualised. Users are paying for the product behind SUN, and paying more; that is the hardest demand signal to fake. TVL is leaking out: SUN now holds $506.09M, -19% over 30 days, -7% over 90; annualised fees equal 3.9% of TVL. TVL is the ceiling on SUN's fees, and the ceiling is coming down.

SUN's 30-day protocol revenue is $187.31K, only about 12% of fees; holders received about $46.33K (25% of revenue). Most fees go to LPs or operators; SUN holders see little cash flow.

For positioning: On-chain, SUN has the bright spot of "growing its fees" and the worry of "leaking TVL".
