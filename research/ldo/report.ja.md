## 研究結論

Lido and LDO are not the same book. The protocol holds Ethereum stake: DefiLlama TVL about **$24.39B**, roughly **48.7%** of Ethereum’s **$50.08B** chain DeFi, and **18.6×** Rocket Pool’s about **$1.31B**. Thirty-day fees about **$44.95M** versus Rocket Pool about **$2.76M**. The token holds a thin slice: protocol revenue about **$2.79M**, capture about **6.2%**; holder revenue about **$0.78M**. Circulating cap about **$309.5M**, rank **137**, is not in the same band as UNI about **$3.75B** or AAVE about **$1.93B**.

Price about **$0.371**, roughly **95%** below the 20 Aug 2021 ATH of **$7.30**. Thirty days **+28.3%**; seven days **−2.8%** — part of the bounce is already done. Hard cap **1.00B**, circulating about **834.2M** (about 83%). LunarCrush social dominance only **0.028%**, sentiment **58%**. Themes include buybacks and a FalconX-linked sale of about **$4.4M** LDO — weekly mentions +72% is a pulse; monthly mentions **−36%**. AltIndex has no $LDO page; no cashtag is invented.

**Buy score 6.0 / 10 (hold / wait).** The staking book sets a floor. Fee capture and token size cap the upside. Not a sell order.

## ステーキング帳簿：ロックはイーサリアムDeFiのほぼ半分

Lido TVL about **$24.39B**. Ethereum chain DeFi about **$50.08B**, so Lido is about **48.7%** of that book. Versus Rocket Pool about **$1.31B**, roughly **18.6×**; 30-day fees **$44.95M** versus **$2.76M**, about **16.3×**. In liquid staking, scale is not the same table.

Cap / TVL about **0.013×** looks extremely cheap. Do not use that multiple. TVL is other people’s staked ETH, not LDO’s asset and not cash holders can draw. UNI cap about **$3.75B** with 24h volume about **$681M**; AAVE cap about **$1.93B** with volume about **$224M**. LDO cap about **$309.5M** and volume about **$43.24M**, turnover about **14.0%**: the tape is usable, the object is a governance token, not blue-chip DeFi. CoinGecko’s MKR circulating cap is a broken print; it is not used here.

## トークン：4495万ドルの手数料のうち279万ドルだけ

Lido’s 30-day fees about **$44.95M**, protocol revenue about **$2.79M**, capture about **6.2%**. Holder revenue about **$0.78M**, thinner still. Rocket Pool revenue over the same window is **$0** — the rival pays the token even less, which does not make LDO “cheap enough to buy.”

Against a $309.5M cap, annualising 30-day revenue ×12 is about **$33.5M**, cap / revenue about **9.2×**. That multiple only holds if capture does not slip and the fee pool does not shrink. The coin is already **+28.3%** over 30 days and **−2.8%** over seven. A thick fee pool is Lido’s fact. A thin token slice is LDO’s fact.

- **Capture stays thin:** $44.95M of fees leave $2.79M of revenue. A fat fee pool is not automatically LDO cash flow.
- **Cap/TVL misleads:** 0.013× treats other people’s ETH as the token’s asset.
- **Sells are a public theme:** LunarCrush logged a FalconX-linked ~$4.4M LDO print; sentiment is only 58%.
- **Bounce already printed:** +28.3% in 30 days, already giving back over 7 days.
- **No checkable cashtag series:** AltIndex has no page; attention is dominance only, and dominance is near zero.
