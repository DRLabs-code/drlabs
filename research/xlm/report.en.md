## On-chain usage: seeing on-chain trading heat up

On-chain trading on Stellar is heating up: $96.94M of 30-day DEX volume, +108% month on month, about 0.4× its TVL.

Stellar DeFi TVL is $248.44M, #19 of 300 chains on DefiLlama, +2% over 30 days and +10% over 90; stablecoins on chain $961.90M. XLM's ecosystem is moving sideways, no clear migration in or out.

Stellar chain-level fees were $48.71K over 30 days, +20% month on month, revenue $48.71K. Annualised it is under 0.1% of market cap, so however fast it moves it is a rounding error against $6.67B.

Desk view: On-chain data sides with XLM; demand is growing for real.

## Narrative and seat: seated in Smart Contract Platform

CoinGecko files XLM under Smart Contract Platform, Layer 1, Real World Assets (RWA), ecosystems Stellar Ecosystem. The project's own CoinGecko description reads: “Stellar is an open-source, decentralized network co-founded in 2014 by Jed McCaleb and Joyce Kim to facilitate the fast and low-cost transfer of value across different currencies and assets globally.” That is XLM's self-positioning, not our assessment. The sector signal is split: XLM's sector has a 30-day median of +6.6% and a cap-weighted (ex-self) change of +24.6%, against Bitcoin's +8.4%; the head and the tail of the peer group are not moving together.

Desk view: XLM's sector gives no single direction: neither a tailwind nor a headwind.

## Token and supply: inflating its circulating supply fast

XLM's circulating supply rose about +10% in a year, +2.8% in the last 90 days; that dilution has to be netted out of any valuation comparison.

About $2.82B of XLM is not yet circulating (70% float, FDV $9.49B). XLM's unlock tail is short, but its timing needs tracking.

CoinGecko shows no maximum supply for XLM; long-run value depends on net issuance after burns, not a fixed scarcity number.

Desk view: XLM's supply keeps diluting holders; any buy case has to clear that first.

## Market structure: thinly traded, with liquidity a hard constraint

Turnover is only about 1.7% ($116.01M (peer median about 4.5%); 7-day average volume is -49% vs the prior 30 days); liquidity is a hard constraint for XLM: easy to get in, hard to get out on schedule.

XLM's averages contradict each other: 200-day +4%, 50-day -3% (spot $0.190). Setups like XLM's usually end with a decisive break one way.

Desk view: XLM's tape is a headwind; patience beats courage here.
