## Token and supply: inflating its circulating supply fast

On CoinGecko's count, M's circulating supply changed about +35% in a year. A passive M holder is diluted by that much; price must beat it before it is a real gain.

M's circulating cap is $1.99B vs FDV $4.71B: about 23% is circulating. At this price the locked M remainder is worth about $6.69B, 3.4× the float. Unlocks are standing supply for M unless demand outgrows emissions.

DefiLlama's unlock schedule shows about 56.11M M unlocking in the next 30 days, 2.5% of circulating supply, roughly $48.70M at spot; the next cliff is on 2026-11-01, about 7.64M tokens (0.3% of float, insiders). That is M supply you can put on a calendar.

Our read: M's supply keeps diluting holders; any buy case has to clear that first.

## Market structure: lagging Bitcoin over 30 days

M is -33.0% over 30 days vs Bitcoin's -6.0%, far behind by 27.1%. Money is elsewhere; M needs its own reason to win it back.

At $0.868, M trails its 200-day by 55% and 19% under the 50-day. Trend followers will not buy M here; support comes mainly from contrarian money.

M did $3.95M in 24h volume, only about 0.2% turnover (peer median about 18.3%); 7-day average volume is +65% vs the prior 30 days. Institutional size would move M's price, which is itself a reason for a discount.

Our read: M's price structure is weak; bottom-fishing has poor odds.

## カタリストとリスク

### Main risks

- Unlock calendar: M unlocks about 2.5% of float in the next 30 days (~$48.70M); next cliff 2026-11-01.
- Dilution: M added about 35% to circulation in a year; holders must beat that first.
- Liquidity: M trades only about $3.95M a day; size will see real slippage.
- M is a meme asset; when attention moves on, there is no fundamental floor.
- Macro and policy: like all crypto, M trades with global liquidity and regulation; single-coin work cannot hedge that.

**What would change our view on M:** M reclaiming the 200-day ($1.937), how the 2026-11-01 unlock is absorbed, 30-day strength vs Bitcoin (now -27%) flipping.
