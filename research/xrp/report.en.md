## Research take

XRP’s cap seat and the on-chain book we can check do not match. Circulating mcap is about **$85.91B**, CoinGecko rank **5**, behind Bitcoin about **$1.55T** and Ethereum about **$308.16B**, then BNB about **$96.85B**. 24h volume is about **$994.26M**, turnover about **1.2%**: a bit thicker than BNB’s about **$772.51M** (about 0.8% turnover), but only a sliver of Bitcoin’s about **$15.17B** and about one-eighth of Ethereum’s about **$8.05B**. The tape is reserve-like, not a Solana-style execution venue.

Price about **$1.37**, roughly **62%** below the 17 Jul 2025 ATH of **$3.65**. Thirty days **+35.5%**; seven days already **−3.3%** — the easy leg is done. Hard cap about **100B**; circulating about **62.88B** / total about **99.99B**, roughly **63%** outside, FDV about **$136.60B**, about **59%** thicker than the float. LunarCrush social dominance **3.10%**, sentiment **82%**. AltIndex (updated 11 Sep 2026) prints about **299** daily $XRP cashtags versus about **280** for $BTC in the same sample — discussion does not lose to Bitcoin.

**Buy score 5.8 / 10 (hold / wait).** Liquidity and real heat set a floor. No fee ledger and a 30-day bounce already in the print cap the upside. Not a sell order.

## Rank five: the tape can match BNB, not the seat

A rank-5 ticket prints only about **$1.0B** of 24h volume. Bitcoin the same day is about **$15.17B**, Ethereum about **$8.05B**. XRP only wins the tape against BNB: $994M versus $773M. Turnover about **1.2%** sits in the same zone as Bitcoin’s about **1.0%** (15.17 / 1550). That is a reserve tape, not a high-turnover execution layer.

Writing Ethereum, Solana or BSC DEX prints into an XRP “ecosystem” is the wrong book. Those are other venues’ execution ledgers. XRP’s own seat is this: the cap already paid for a cross-border settlement and compliance story; volume only proves the ticket still trades, not that a fee ledger exists.

## On-chain: there is no checkable fee ledger

In this snapshot, DefiLlama chain TVL that we can check is Ethereum about **$50.16B**, Solana about **$5.90B**, BSC about **$5.65B**, Base about **$5.59B**, Tron about **$5.50B**. 30-day DEX volume is Solana about **$73.95B**, Ethereum about **$39.76B**, BSC about **$37.21B**, Base about **$27.72B**. XRP Ledger is not in those cells.

So XRP cannot be written as “a DeFi chain whose data is just missing for now.” What is missing is not a footnote. It is the whole settlement book. Buying XRP is buying a payments network and a regulatory story, not lockup, parked stables or chain fees. FDV sits about **$50.7B** above the float; the uncirculated **~37%** will keep pressing the multiple through unlocks or escrow.

Take: until a fee ledger shows up, rank five explains liquidity and narrative, not cash flow.

## Main risks

- **Narrative premium:** rank 5 is buying cross-border settlement and compliance, not checkable TVL or fees. If the story loosens, rank can fall first.
- **Bounce already spent:** +35.5% in 30 days, already negative over 7. Near-term looks like risk appetite, not a fee step-up.
- **Unlocks and FDV:** about 63% circulating, FDV about $136.6B. The rest will keep pressing the multiple.
- **Heat fading:** if $XRP mentions fall clearly below same-vintage $BTC, the attention premium can leave fast.
- **Reading the wrong chain:** folding ETH or SOL DEX or TVL into an XRP “ecosystem” misplaces the seat.
