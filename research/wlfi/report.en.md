## Token and supply: mostly not yet circulating

Only about 32% of WLFI circulates (cap $1.72B, FDV $5.41B). The remaining ~$3.69B of WLFI, 2.1× the float, usually sits with team, investors and ecosystem funds whose cost is far below spot.

On CoinGecko's count, WLFI's circulating supply changed about +17% in a year, +0.0% in the last 90 days. A passive WLFI holder is diluted by that much; price must beat it before it is a real gain.

Desk view: Supply is the biggest structural weight on WLFI; the valuation needs a discount.
