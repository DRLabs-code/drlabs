## Catalysts and risks

### Possible catalysts

- BTW leads its sector by +164% over 30 days; momentum inside a sector tends to persist.

### Main risks

- Locked supply: about $17.04B of BTW (5.6× the float) has yet to reach the market.
- Cooling demand: BTW's 30-day fees are -22% MoM.
- Outflows: BTW's TVL is -52% over 30 days.
- Overextension: BTW is 529% above its 200-day; mean reversion is itself a risk.
- High volatility: BTW runs about 281% annualised (~14.7% a day); stops and size must be set for that range.
- Macro and policy: like all crypto, BTW trades with global liquidity and regulation; single-coin work cannot hedge that.

**What would change our view on BTW:** BTW losing the 200-day ($0.319), 30-day fee momentum flipping from -22%, a change in unlock pace or buyback policy, 30-day strength vs Bitcoin (now +168%) flipping.
