## Token and supply: inflating its circulating supply fast

On CoinGecko's count, CRO's circulating supply changed about +41% in a year, +7.3% in the last 90 days. A passive CRO holder is diluted by that much; price must beat it before it is a real gain.

About 49% of CRO's supply is still to be mined (51% issued, 50.73B of 100.00B), worth about $2.75B at spot. CRO's schedule is transparent, but it does not stop.

For positioning: Supply is the biggest structural weight on CRO; the valuation needs a discount.

For positioning: On-chain data is cooling CRO; the valuation needs to follow.
