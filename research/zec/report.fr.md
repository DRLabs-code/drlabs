At $1,199 it sits 92% above the 200-day average and 3% above the 50-day. The medium-term trend is up, so dips are more likely to be bought.

30-day annualised volatility about 136%, 90-day max drawdown -27%. For the same risk budget the position should be a fraction of a Bitcoin position.

Our read: The tape is helping; going with it is easier than fighting it.
