## Conclusion

Split Ethereum into two ledgers and the conclusion changes. The settlement ledger is still its: DefiLlama chain TVL is about **$50.16B**, roughly **56.7%** of all chains; stablecoins are about **$146.9B**, above Tron’s ~$93.8B and Solana’s ~$16.1B. The execution ledger is not: 30-day DEX volume is about **$72.48B** on Solana versus **$38.78B** on Ethereum; 30-day chain fees are about **$22.70M** versus **$11.38M**. Market cap is about **$306.5B**, **5.1×** Solana’s ~$59.8B. The token is priced as the place dollars sit, not as the place tickets turn over.

Price is about **$2,511**, roughly **49%** below the 24 Aug 2025 ATH of **$4,946**. The last 30 days are about **+33.8%**; the last 7 days only **+2.4%**. ETH/BTC is about **0.0326**, and ETH is about **19.8%** of Bitcoin’s cap. On X, AltIndex (updated 10 Sep) prints about **198** daily $ETH cashtag mentions versus **356** for $BTC. ETH’s own monthly average fell from about 619 in April to 217 in August. Sharpe Google Trends (11 Sep) has Bitcoin at 56/100 and Ethereum at 8/100. Attention is still a Bitcoin tape.

**Buy score 6.4 / 10 (hold / wait).** Settlement quality sets a floor. Leaking flow and a 30-day bounce cap the upside. Not a sell order, and not an add.

## Règlement : collatéral et dollars restent ici

Chain TVL: Ethereum about **$50.16B**, Solana **$5.89B**, BSC **$5.65B**, Base **$5.59B**, Tron **$5.49B**, Bitcoin **$4.18B**. Ethereum is larger than the next five combined. Stables are starker: Ethereum about **$146.9B**, Tron about **$93.8B** (a transfer rail), Solana about **$16.1B**, BSC about **$13.3B**. Who parks dollars and blue-chip collateral is still Ethereum.

Market cap / TVL is about **6.1×**. Bitcoin on the same cut is about **371×**, Solana about **10.2×**. That does not make ETH “cheap” — TVL is not holder cash flow — but the multiple is not a meme multiple against its own book.

## Exécution : Solana et les L2 sont les lieux actifs

30-day DEX volume: Solana about **$72.48B**, Ethereum **$38.78B**, BSC **$36.66B**, Base **$27.06B**. 24h follows the same order: Solana about **$2.92B**, Ethereum **$1.42B**. Fees rhyme: Solana about **$22.70M** over 30 days, Ethereum **$11.38M**. Ethereum’s protocol-style revenue is about **$2.63M** versus Solana’s **$2.35M** — capture is fine, the pool is smaller.

Base TVL is about **$5.59B**, already next to Solana. Retail flow leaves L1 for Ethereum’s own L2s and for Solana. A fatter settlement layer does not automatically thicken ETH holder fees.

## Risques principaux

- **Flow keeps leaving:** Solana’s 30-day DEX book is already ~1.9× Ethereum. A wider gap reprices the settlement premium.
- **L2 leakage:** Base TVL is already next to Solana. Fees stay with sequencers and apps, not automatically with ETH.
- **Bounce prepaid:** +33.8% in 30 days while X chatter cooled is risk appetite, not a fee step-change.
- **ETFs are pipes:** One-day net outflows and share rotation between products are not persistent spot bid.
- **No hard cap:** Supply is uncapped. That is not Dogecoin-style issuance, and it is not Bitcoin scarcity either.
