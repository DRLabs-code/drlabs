## On-chain usage: seeing its fees shrink

Rocket Pool's fees are shrinking: $887.46K over 30 days, -69% vs the prior 30; at the last 7 days' pace the monthly run-rate is -80% (~$10.80M annualised). With revenue falling, a low multiple on RPL can still be a value trap.

TVL is leaking out: Rocket Pool now holds $1.08B, -16% over 30 days, +8% over 90; annualised fees equal 1.0% of TVL. TVL is the ceiling on RPL's fees, and the ceiling is coming down.

RPL's value capture is thin: Rocket Pool keeps only about 0% of fees as revenue (30-day $0).

For positioning: RPL's fundamentals are weakening; price will reflect it sooner or later.

## Market structure: still pinned under its 200-day average

At $1.370, RPL is still 17% short of the 200-day and 19% under the 50-day; it is in a downtrend, and bottom-fishing costs time.

Against Bitcoin, RPL lost 14.8% of relative ground in 30 days (-22.4% vs -7.6%). Persistent underperformance like RPL's often shows up before the bad fundamental news does.

Turnover is only about 1.3% ($419.51K (peer median about 7.8%); 7-day average volume is -50% vs the prior 30 days); liquidity is a hard constraint for RPL: easy to get in, hard to get out on schedule.

For positioning: The tape is unfriendly to RPL; talk sizing once the trend repairs.
