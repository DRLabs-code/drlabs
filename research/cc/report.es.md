## Token and supply: essentially fully circulating

About 100% of CC already circulates (cap $4.23B, FDV $4.23B); price is plain supply and demand, not an unlock calendar.

Per DefiLlama's schedule, CC releases about 4.9% of its float over 90 days (1.94B tokens, ~$205.89M), a mild, steady supply.

CC has no supply cap; scarcity has to be earned through burns and demand growth, not written into the rules.

For positioning: CC's supply side cuts both ways: essentially fully circulating, but uncapped in supply; net them together.
